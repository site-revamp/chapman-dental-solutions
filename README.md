Chapman Dental Solutions Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
Chapman Dental Solutions is an independent dental practice based in Whickham, Newcastle upon Tyne, specialising in dentures, dental implants, restorative dentistry and general dental care.

Originally established as a dental prosthetics laboratory by Ross Chapman in 2008, the business has developed into a dental practice offering clinical and specialist dental treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning Chapman Dental Solutions as a specialist provider of personalised denture and implant treatments while clearly presenting its wider dental services. It targets patients seeking bespoke dentures, implant-supported solutions, cosmetic treatments and comprehensive dental care across Newcastle and the wider North East.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Specialist Treatment Presentation
- Improved the organisation and presentation of specialist services, including bespoke dentures, implant-retained dentures, full-arch implant bridges and Teeth in 1 Day.
- Created clearer distinctions between denture solutions, implant treatments, cosmetic dentistry and general dental care.
- Gave greater visibility to the practice's specialist dental technology background.
- Addresses the challenge of communicating the company's specialist expertise alongside its broader dental services.
2. Stronger Business Identity and Clinical Positioning
- Strengthened the presentation of Chapman Dental Solutions as a dental practice built on its dental prosthetics laboratory heritage.
- Refined the messaging around clinical dental technology, personalised treatment and specialist restorative solutions.
- Improved the communication of the relationship between its laboratory expertise and patient-facing dental services.
- Addresses the need to differentiate the practice from general dental providers and communicate its specialist background more effectively.
3. Improved Homepage Structure and Patient Journey
- Reorganised homepage content to create a clearer progression through specialist treatments, general dental services and practice information.
- Improved the presentation of important patient contact and location information.
- Refined the content hierarchy to help visitors identify relevant treatments and understand how to contact the practice.
- Addresses the challenge of navigating a broad treatment offering and finding relevant information efficiently.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured and professional presentation that reflects the practice's specialist dental positioning.

Key improvements include:
- Visual presentation: A refined appearance that communicates clinical expertise and professional dental care.
- Layout: Better organisation of specialist treatments, supporting services and practice information.
- Typography: Clearer heading hierarchy and improved readability across homepage sections.
- Consistency: More cohesive presentation of the practice's identity, treatment categories and business messaging.
- User experience: A more logical browsing journey for patients exploring dentures, implants and other dental treatments.
- Content hierarchy: Greater emphasis on specialist denture and implant solutions while maintaining visibility for general dentistry.

5. Project Objective
The objective of the homepage redesign is to strengthen Chapman Dental Solutions' online positioning as a specialist dental practice with a strong foundation in dental prosthetics.

The updated direction prioritises specialist treatment visibility, clearer clinical positioning, organised service presentation and an accessible patient journey while communicating the practice's expertise in bespoke dentures, dental implants and comprehensive dental care.
